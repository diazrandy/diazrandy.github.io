# Randy Díaz - Personal Portfolio & Online Resume

Welcome to the repository for my personal website: [diazrandy.github.io](https://diazrandy.github.io).

## 📖 About This Project
This project serves as my digital portfolio and interactive resume. It outlines my professional trajectory of over 10 years in Retail Operations, detailing my leadership roles at top global brands including Apple, adidas, IKEA, H&M, and Zara. The website also highlights my academic background and current transition into tech.

## ✨ Key Features
* **Bilingual Support:** The content is structured to support both Spanish and English.
* **Experience Timeline:** A detailed history of my career milestones, including international store openings, team leadership (15+ people), and KPI management.
* **Downloadable Assets:** Direct access to download my updated CV in PDF format (both in Spanish and English).
* **Responsive Design:** Optimized for reading across different devices and screen sizes.
